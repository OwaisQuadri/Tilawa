## TIL-4: Haptic Feedback

**Settings Toggle**
1. Open Settings tab → verify new "Haptics" section with "Haptic Feedback" toggle
2. Toggle defaults to ON

**Page Swipes (Mushaf)**
1. Swipe between mushaf pages → feel a light tap on each page turn

**Playback Controls (Mini Player)**
1. Start playback, then tap Play/Pause → feel a medium tap
2. Tap Skip Forward / Skip Backward → feel a light tap each
3. Tap Stop (X) → feel a light tap

**Long-Press Ayah (Mushaf)**
1. Long-press an ayah on the mushaf page → feel a medium tap when the context menu appears

**Annotation Editor**
1. Open a recording's annotation editor
2. Tap the "Marker" button to add a marker → feel a medium tap
3. Drag a marker handle along the waveform → feel subtle selection ticks during drag
4. Tap "Finalize" and confirm → feel a warning buzz on the destructive confirmation

**Destructive Actions**
1. Settings → Clear Cache → confirm "Delete All Cached Audio" → feel a warning buzz
2. Long-press an ayah → "Remove Bookmark" → feel a warning buzz
3. Playback setup → delete a sliding window preset → feel a warning buzz

**Toggle OFF**
1. Go to Settings → turn Haptic Feedback OFF
2. Repeat all interactions above → verify NO haptic feedback fires
3. Turn it back ON → verify haptics resume
