## TIL-3: Bismillah Before Each Surah

**Basic Functionality**
1. Open playback setup, set range to Surah 2 (Al-Baqarah) starting from ayah 1
2. Tap Play → verify Bismillah audio plays before ayah 1, then Al-Baqarah ayah 1 plays
3. During Bismillah playback, verify the UI shows "Al-Baqarah - 1" as current ayah (not "Al-Fatihah - 1")

**Excluded Surahs**
1. Play from Surah 1 (Al-Fatihah) ayah 1 → no duplicate Bismillah (1:1 IS the Bismillah)
2. Play from Surah 9 (At-Tawbah) ayah 1 → no Bismillah (Islamic tradition)

**Mid-Surah Start**
1. Play from Surah 2 ayah 5 → no Bismillah (only plays at surah start)

**Ayah Repeats**
1. Set ayah repeat to 3, play Surah 2 ayah 1
2. Verify: Bismillah plays once, then ayah 1 repeats 3 times (Bismillah does NOT repeat)

**Toggle Setting**
1. In playback setup sheet, find "Bismillah before surah" toggle in Playback section
2. Toggle OFF, play Surah 2 ayah 1 → no Bismillah plays
3. Toggle ON, play again → Bismillah plays

**Skip Controls**
1. Play a range crossing surah boundaries (e.g. end of Surah 2 into Surah 3)
2. Skip forward into Surah 3 ayah 1 → Bismillah plays
3. Skip backward to Surah 3 ayah 1 → Bismillah plays again
